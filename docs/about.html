About

What this is

On This Day in Yogscast History is a fan-made site. The home page shows every main-channel Yogscast video uploaded on today's date, going all the way back through the channel's history — whatever year it happened to be. Check back tomorrow and it's a different set of videos entirely. Click the video in the middle to play it, and with Autoplay on it'll carry on through the rest of the day's videos by itself.

Custom Filter

The Custom Filter page lets you dig through the whole archive yourself: search video titles, pick any date of the year, and filter by game or by series — from Shadow of Israphel and Jaffa Factory to Blood on the Clocktower and Tiny Teams. Mix and match them, sort the results, and play anything straight from the page. Games and series are worked out from the video titles, so the odd one may be in the wrong place.

Where the data comes from

Every video's title, description, view count, upload date and duration is pulled from the official YouTube Data API, using the Yogscast's public uploads. A small automated job checks for new uploads once a day, so the site should pick up new videos without anyone having to do anything by hand.

Shorts

YouTube Shorts uploaded on today's date get their own “Shorts on this day” section further down the home page, separate from the main carousel and grid above it, since they're a different kind of video to browse.

Not affiliated with The Yogscast

This is an independent fan project, built for fun, and isn't run by or officially connected to The Yogscast in any way. All video titles, thumbnails and content remain the property of their original creators — this site just links out to them on YouTube.

Analytics and your privacy

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of it get used — for example how many people visit, which pages they view, and which searches, filters and videos are popular. It doesn't collect your name or contact details, and the numbers are only used to improve the site. Google Analytics stores a few cookies in your browser to do this, and Google processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced (“nocookie”) player, which only starts storing anything once you press play.
